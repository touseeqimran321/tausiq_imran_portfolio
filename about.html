Design Creed

“A great design is not just seen—it is remembered. Every line, typeface, and color must serve a conscious communicative purpose.”

I am Areesha Amjad, a passionate Graphic Designer with a strong interest in visual communication, creative design, and digital media. I completed my Intermediate in Computer Science (ICS) from Punjab Group of Colleges and am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) at Lahore Garrison University.

I enjoy transforming ideas into visually engaging designs that communicate effectively and leave a lasting impression. My creative interests include graphic design, branding, digital illustration, and developing attractive visual content. I am committed to improving my design skills, exploring new creative techniques, and continuously expanding my professional expertise.

By pairing analytical computer science training with an intuitive sense for aesthetics and color harmony, I bring structured precision to creative compositions—ensuring projects are as thoughtfully organized as they are striking to behold.
